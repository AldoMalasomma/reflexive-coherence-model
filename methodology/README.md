# Methodology: Reflexive Coherence Index v0.1

## Overview

The Reflexive Coherence Index (RCI) is a proposed operational approach for examining selected properties of reflexive coherence described by the Reflexive Coherence Model (RCM). RCI v0.1 translates parts of that theoretical framework into observable functional measures; it does not determine whether a system is conscious.

## RCI v0.1 Components

RCI v0.1 documents five components, each tied to logged dynamics, predictions, interventions, or perturbation responses:

- **State/self-model alignment (A):** examines how accurately a system’s self-model represents relevant aspects of its current or recent global state.
- **Incremental predictive utility (P):** examines whether using the self-model improves prediction beyond a baseline predictor that does not use it.
- **Causal influence (C):** examines whether the self-model or reflexive feedback causally affects subsequent state transitions under controlled intervention or ablation.
- **Temporal persistence (T):** examines whether alignment, predictive utility, and causal influence remain stable across a defined time window rather than appearing as isolated spikes.
- **Recovery after perturbation (R):** examines whether a defined reflexive configuration degrades under perturbation and recovers toward its pre-perturbation functional level.

These components require explicit observables and known failure modes. The presence of a module alone does not increase a component’s score.

## Methodological Approach

Project Janus explores RCI v0.1 in deterministic synthetic environments. Its documented approach uses simple baselines, controlled interventions, ablations, matched counterfactual comparisons, and reproducible traces to examine functional dynamics under specified conditions.

The components are compared with appropriate baseline measures. Counterfactual and ablation comparisons are used to assess whether self-modeling or feedback contributes causally, while perturbation protocols examine temporal persistence and recovery. The operational specification is provisional; this page summarizes its approach rather than reproducing the full internal specification.

## Interpretation Boundaries

RCI components can describe selected functional patterns within controlled conditions, such as alignment, predictive contribution, causal influence, persistence, or recovery. They measure observed dynamics, not the presence of particular modules.

RCI v0.1 is not a consciousness score. A higher value is not evidence of subjective experience, sentience, or awareness. Comparisons are meaningful only within the relevant controlled conditions, and results should be treated as metric or system findings rather than conclusions about experience.

## Research Status

RCI v0.1 is an experimental operational implementation, not a validated measure of consciousness or subjective experience. Its components, baselines, validity conditions, and performance require further empirical and computational investigation. The documented M1 and M2 summaries and their limitations are in [Experiments](../experiments/README.md).
